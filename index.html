01 now

What I build at Syllotips

Syllotips lets enterprise AI agents learn from a company's own experts. I work on the engine underneath: how agents plan, which tools they call, how they cite their sources, and how we find where they fail. Most of it is the , not the model.

tools

MCP layer

Connects the agents to enterprise tools such as Atlassian, HubSpot and Intercom: one architecture for cloud and private cloud, OAuth handling, and tool results the agents can cite.

compute

Code interpreter

A sandbox where agents analyse spreadsheets and produce reports and files, on cloud and private cloud alike.

experts

Assistant agent

Helps experts close the loop on issues: it investigates what went wrong and proposes a solution for them to review.

serving

Self-hosted models

vLLM on multi-GPU clusters: open-weight LLMs with up to 1M-token context, plus small decision models for fast routing and grading. The agents can run fully on our own infrastructure.

fine-tuning

Small models, on a supercomputer

Fine-tuned open-weight LLMs from 0.6B to 32B so that one small model replaces three commercial LLM calls for answer confidence estimation.

03 research

Do models mean what they say?

Factuality, word senses, and multilingual and multimodal meaning, mostly with the Sapienza NLP group. Published at ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, EACL and in Computational Linguistics. Reviewer for ACL, EMNLP, EACL and ICLR.

  1. Computational Linguistics · 2026 · co-first author

    Truth or Mirage? LLM-Oasis

    A large-scale resource for end-to-end factuality evaluation, built by extracting and falsifying facts from Wikipedia.

    codearXiv

  2. ACL · 2024 · first author

    Word Sense Linking

    Finds the ambiguous words in raw text and links each one to its sense, with no pre-marked spans or candidate lists.

    codemodel

  3. EMNLP · 2025

    Concept-pedia

    A wide-coverage, semantically annotated multimodal dataset of 165K+ visual concepts built on BabelNet, with the Concept-10k benchmark for vision-language models.

    pdfdataset

  4. Findings of NAACL · 2025

    Vocabulary adaptation for Italian LLMs

    Part of the Minerva work: adapting the vocabulary cuts token fertility, so the same model reads more Italian per token.
